Techno-juggling

October 2024 - Present

Photos from Showing with Ethnographic Media Lab, April 2026

Commentary

Techno-juggling engages audiences with a historical account of juggling’s role in shaping the imagination of new computer technologies. These technological periods include 1980s computer graphics juggling animations, 1990s siteswap juggling simulators, 2010s camera-based ball tracking, and contemporary approaches including mobile phone apps, sensor infused balls, juggling-automatons, and VR juggling performances. This history is told interactively for players as they roll mini-tennis balls on an live-coding inspired juggling-table.
