Leveling the Playing Field in the Social Sciences

April 2024

This research essay was inspired by my own past experiments with automated webscraping and crawling as well as conversations with collegues on algorithmic nature of multimedia web and app recommender interfaces. The research investigated existing techniques called agent-based tests in the field of computational journalism. I looked at experiments carried out by Nicholas Diakopoulos to evaluate social media's treatment of journalism from major news sources. These techniques were studied to better understand how to replicate the methods as well as build awareness for challenges and limitations of the method.
