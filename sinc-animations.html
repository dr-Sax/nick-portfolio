Sinc Animations

2024

Commentary

These animations represent an exploration into mathematically generated abstract animations. The Sinc function (commonly used in physics and electrical engineering), was used to construct image frames of wave interferences. While these are flat images, the wave's depth is mapped to the rgb color channels giving such explosive lava lamp like colors. I experimented by varrying structural, quantity, and motional characteristics of 3d Sinc waves to uncover different feelings in the animations. Such structures based on spirals and concentric circles as well as differential (relative) changes in sinc wave amplitude and decay constants led to most interesting animations.
