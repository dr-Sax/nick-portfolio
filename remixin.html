Commentary

This mixed media installation invites viewers into a layered experience that bridges physical and digital realms. What appears as a static paper artwork transforms when approached with a smartphone—the very device we instinctively use to capture and preserve our encounters with art.

By scanning the collaged QR code, visitors unlock an interactive dimension where the artwork becomes animated, revealing the video collage’s construction process with an accompanying audio mashup. This augmented reality experience adapts to each viewer's unique perspective, maintaining the digital elements' relationship to the physical piece regardless of viewing angle.

Inspired by observations as a gallery guard, this work explores our habitual gesture of photographing art—a modern ritual of replication that speaks to how we process the unfamiliar. The smartphone serves not merely as a documentation tool but as a mediator between viewer and artwork, becoming an essential part of the experience itself.

The visual and sonic elements draw from jazz history and its intersections with social movements, creating a rhythmic dialogue between past and present. Through this multi-sensory engagement, the piece invites contemplation of how technology shapes our perception of art and how cultural context influences our understanding of creative expression.
