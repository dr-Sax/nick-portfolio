Pythagorean Polygons

January 2023

Commentary

This project was a Python-OpenGL computer animation of a large number of overlapping and moving polygons. I created this visual as an experimentation of the ways in which differential motion of shapes can construct an inviting illusion for the user. Inspired by John Whitney's writings and early experimental animations in digital harmony, I sought to apply his principles to discover new visual harmonies. I designed this graphic by placing polygons in a 2D grid and varying their sizes by the Pythagorean theorem. The final result is both calming and immersive to the viewer.
