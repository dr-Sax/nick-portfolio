Renaissance Box

June 2025 - September 2025

Link to Renaissance Box Website

I was awarded a $1000 grant through a local incubator called Renaissance Box for interactive media installations at Cleveland's Ingenuity Festival and CIA’s Interactive Media Lab. In that time, I served as project Leader on a team with a mixed media artist and high school student. I engaged in iterative design including concept mapping, rapid prototyping, field research, and playtesting with local participants.

The Exhibit

Collage-Bop: Participants create video collages by manipulating air hockey pucks and playing a MIDI keyboard. The puck’s positions are tracked by an overhead camera which is mapped to a user-facing screen. Users can also control audio-visual aspects like volume, shape, size, color, layer, speed, and playback position. Users receive code and link to a website where they can replay the construction process of their video-collage.

Tell-A-Vision: a human figure composed in the style of Nam June Paik’s TV Robots. Constructed using CRT TVs, computer monitors, old telephones, cameras, and mobile phones. Display monitors are located on each limb and are programmable based on participant movements.
