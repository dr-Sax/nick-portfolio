Commentary

For this second workshop, I focused on trying to better illustrate what I thought and wanted to communicate about Leger’s 1950 collection of drawings and poetry entitled Cirque. I first went through his hand written poetry and used Claude to translate them to english. I landed upon a section regarding machines and assembly line workers and knew it should be the focal point. From their, I fragmented parts of his other poetry together into a short narrative about going to the circus and recorded the audio aloud. I was surprised to learn that much of this writing is also compiled in his book called the functions of painting. Next, I started juggling while listing to the audio and mapped out a routine or set of tricks which I thought imitates the progression of the narrative, especially in trying to contrast mechanical or rectangular movements with circular ones. I used premiere to cut up the video and align it with audio to convey the story Third, I tried out a juggling specific CV script to track yellow tint from the balls to create the flows or traces of color you see following the balls. The creator of this script has worked with visualizing juggling extensively and has several youtube videos. Fourth, I pushed the video through with a circular text animator which I made for a previous assignment in a mashup culture course here at the New School. Lastly, I moved all of these media objects into blippar and anchored using a printout of the Leger painting which was taped up to my wall. I added a tapping functionality where you can tap the description of the painting and it pulls up the original french text which I gained inspiration from.
