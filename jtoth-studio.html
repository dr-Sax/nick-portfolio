Commentary

I collaborated with Jason Toth, a photographer, generative AI video animator, and digital artist to create projection mapping experiences in his studio space at 78th Street Studios in Cleveland, OH. These realtime systems were made with TouchDesigner to provide Jason with a way to show his animated works to studio guests. We first started with gridded flowing animations on his windows as they are visible to people passing by outside the building. We then moved into his listening room to map onto a semi-circular wooden wall fixture. We explored regional mapping where individual videos could be placed onto each surface, but eventually found it was easiest for him to connect his phone as a video stream and queue up animations from his YouTube Channel to show guests. Last, Audiovisual reactive waveforms were mapped onto the wooden borders which linked to the selected audio input in the video.
