Commentary

For 2 years, I practiced regularly, performed, and recorded 2 albums with the groups Rent For Cheryl and Cadillac Loverboy from Akron, OH. We performed at many places in Akron and Cleveland including Musica, a year long residence at Baxter's Speakeasy, Beachland Tavern, the Coda, Porchrokr, Crobar, and Rialto Theater. Also, the songs were recorded into two albums at Akron Recording Center for which we also played at their 5 year anniversery party. I performed on alto and tenor saxophones as well as flute. This opportunity gave me a chance to hone my skills as a musician and performer as well contribute to community arts and teamwork and sacrifice to complete the two album projects.
