Commentary

This project involved creating a 3D model of a 1920s Broadway theatre marquee and integrating it into a mobile augmented reality (AR) application using Niantic's 8th Wall Software. The goal was to recreate the iconic look and feel of vintage theater signage, complete with intricate details and period-appropriate typography. The AR experience allows users to view the marquee in their real-world environment through their mobile devices, providing an immersive glimpse into the past. This project required skills in 3D modeling, texturing, and AR development, as well as an understanding of historical design elements.
